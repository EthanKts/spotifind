Spotifind

Spotifind is a Mac app for music discovery. Pick a song, album, artist or genre and it finds music that sounds like it, and every recommendation comes with a short “why it matches” explanation, from shared listeners to groove, mood and energy.

  • Rapid-fire: rate songs one at a time with 30-second previews; every rating sharpens what comes next.
  • Playlists: build one in the style, energy and length you want, then save it to Spotify.
  • Explore a genre: find songs you’ll like even in a genre you don’t usually listen to.
