Spotifind

Spotify is where you listen. Spotifind is where you discover music you’ll love — recommendations that match a song’s groove, mood and feel, playlists of hidden gems, and one-click saving to your Spotify.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.

1 Install

  1. Drag Spotifind into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. Because it’s a personal app (not from the App Store), macOS blocks it the first time and says it can’t verify the developer. Click Done, then:
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about Spotifind and click Open Anyway, then confirm. You only do this once.

If macOS instead says the app “is damaged”, open the Terminal app, paste this and press Return, then open Spotifind again:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Spotifind.app

2 Get your free Last.fm API key

Spotifind’s recommendations come from Last.fm listening data, and everyone uses their own free key. Spotifind asks for it on first launch.

  1. Go to last.fm/api/account/create and log in — or create a free Last.fm account first.
  2. Fill in Application name (for example “Spotifind”) and a short Application description. Leave Callback URL and Application homepage empty. Submit.
  3. Copy the API key (32 letters and numbers). Don’t use the “Shared secret”.
  4. Paste it into Spotifind’s welcome screen and click Start discovering. Spotifind checks it with Last.fm right away.

3 Connect your Spotify (to save playlists)

Spotify only allows this for accounts with Spotify Premium (Spotify’s rule for personal apps since February 2026). Everything else in Spotifind works without it.

  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard, log in with your Spotify account and accept the developer terms.
  2. Click Create app. Name: Spotifind. Description: anything.
  3. Under Redirect URIs add exactly:
    http://127.0.0.1:47320/api/spotify/callback
    and click Add.
  4. Under “Which API/SDKs are you planning to use?” tick Web API, accept the terms and Save.
  5. Open the app’s Settings and copy the Client ID. (You don’t need the client secret.)
  6. In Spotifind, make a playlist (sidebar → Playlists), open it and press Save to Spotify. Paste the Client ID, click Connect Spotify, and approve in the browser page that opens. Come back to Spotifind — it saves the playlist automatically.

After that, Save to Spotify works in one click, and Update on Spotify syncs changes. Your Spotify login stays on your Mac; Disconnect is in the same dialog.

4 Using Spotifind

  • Discover: start from any song, album or artist. Pick a style — Closest Match, Deep Cuts, Hidden Gems, Same Groove, Same Mood, Explore Further — and slide from “Very Similar” to “More Adventurous”.
  • Rate with stars: 4–5★ gets you more like it, 1★ hides it. Hover any artist name to rate the artist. Ratings are how Spotifind learns your taste.
  • Playlists: 10–50 songs in the style you choose, from your taste or one starting point.
  • Open in Spotify jumps straight into the Spotify app. Previews in Spotifind are 30 seconds.

Everything you rate and every key you enter is stored only on your Mac (in ~/Library/Application Support/Spotifind). To uninstall, delete the app and that folder.
