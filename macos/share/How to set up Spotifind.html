Set up Spotifind

Spotifind finds new music based on what you already like, and saves the playlists you make to your Spotify. Setup takes about two minutes.

Step 1

Drag Spotifind to Applications

In the Spotifind window that opened, drag the Spotifind icon onto the Applications folder.

Opened it from the download by mistake? No problem: Spotifind offers to move itself into Applications.

Step 2

Allow it once

  1. Open Spotifind from Applications (or Launchpad).
  2. macOS says it can’t check the app, because it isn’t from the App Store. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to “Spotifind was blocked” and click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.

You only do this once. If macOS says the app “is damaged”, open Terminal, paste this, press Return, and open Spotifind again:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Spotifind.app

Step 3

Follow the setup in the app

Spotifind opens with a short setup: it helps you ask {{SHARED_BY}} to add your Spotify account, logs you in with Spotify (one click, no Premium needed), learns your taste, and shows you around.

You can reopen it any time from Help → Setup Guide…. If something goes wrong, Help → Report a Problem… shows a log file you can send to {{SHARED_BY}}.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later. Your Spotify login stays on your Mac.
