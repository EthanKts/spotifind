Set up Spotifind

Spotifind finds new music based on what you already like, and saves the playlists you make to your Spotify. Setup takes about two minutes.

Before you start

Send {{SHARED_BY}} your Spotify email

Spotify only lets {{SHARED_BY}} share Spotifind with people they’ve added. Send them the email address of your Spotify account (Spotify app → Settings → Account). You don’t need Spotify Premium.

Step 1

Install

  1. Drag Spotifind into your Applications folder, then open it.
  2. macOS will say it can’t check the app, because it isn’t from the App Store. Click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to “Spotifind was blocked” and click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

If macOS says the app “is damaged”, open Terminal, paste this, press Return, and open Spotifind again:
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Spotifind.app

Step 2

Log in with Spotify

Click Log in with Spotify on the welcome screen. Your browser opens Spotify’s page; click Agree, then go back to Spotifind. It learns your taste from what you listen to, and you’re ready.

If the browser says you aren’t added yet, ask {{SHARED_BY}} to add your Spotify email, then click Log in with Spotify again.

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) and macOS 14 or later. Your Spotify login stays on your Mac.
